# ☕ Coffee Sales Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This Power BI project analyzes coffee sales performance using interactive dashboards and business-focused visualizations.

The dashboard is designed to help understand sales trends, product performance, payment patterns, revenue contribution, and overall business performance.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Track overall coffee sales performance
- Identify top-performing coffee products
- Analyze monthly and date-wise revenue trends
- Compare actual revenue against targets
- Understand payment-method patterns
- Support data-driven sales and business decisions

## 📊 Dashboard Pages

### 1. HOME
Provides the landing page and navigation for the Power BI report.

### 2. REVENUE
Focuses on revenue performance and includes analysis by:
- Coffee product
- Date / time trend
- Month
- Actual revenue
- Target revenue

### 3. ABOUT US
Includes additional business information and supporting analysis.

## 🔎 Key Fields Used in the Report

The Power BI report uses fields and measures including:

- `coffee_name`
- `date`
- `Month_name`
- `money`
- `cash_type`
- `card`
- `Total_Revenue`
- `Target_Revenue`
- `Types_coffee`
